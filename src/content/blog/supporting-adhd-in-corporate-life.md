## The Corporate Drain: Why 45-Minute Meetings Are Screaming in an ADHD Brain

If you have ADHD, or if you love someone who does, I want you to remember one important thing: Corporate environments are not built for the ADHD nervous system. Think about the standard corporate meeting. It lasts 45 minutes, maybe an hour. But here is the truth about how an ADHD brain works: You or your loved one can problem-solve that exact same issue in three minutes. Sitting through those extra 42 minutes of corporate fluff? It is literally draining their soul. While everyone else is talking, the ADHD brain is screaming on the inside.

### The Reality of the "After-Work" Crash

It is not easy to navigate life right now. You are trying to be a mom, a partner, a lover—and then you have to subject yourself to this draining corporate environment all day long just because it brings the money in.

By the time you get home, your executive function is completely bankrupt. Guess what happens next?

* You want to drink some wine.
* You want to crash on the sofa.
* You want to do absolutely nothing.

And honestly? Who cares about the unwashed dishes, the piles of clothes, or cleaning the house in that moment? Where is that energy supposed to come from? If this sounds like your reality, please give yourself some grace. And if you are the partner of someone with ADHD, give them some grace, too.

### Moving From "Reactive Mode" to Calm

When you are constantly exhausted, you fall into a consistent reactive mode. You are just surviving, not living.

If you can’t afford to hire outside help, like a cleaning lady to take care of the household chores, you have to find another way. You have to determine what *actually* needs to get done on a daily basis to protect your relationship, your energy, and your peace.

So, how do we calm down? How do we take it easy, prioritize, and stop the overwhelm?

> The Secret: Strip away the complexity. You don’t need ten thousand different apps. You just need a simple, easy list that you can actually follow.

1. Grab a single piece of paper. 2. Draw something simple or write down just two things. Two things that you *need* to do today to help manage your life.

That’s it. Just two things.

### Regulating Your Nervous System

When you simplify your expectations, you help your brain and your entire body calm down and relax. The ADHD nervous system desperately needs to be in a calm state, not stuck in fight-or-flight mode 24/7.

There are absolutely ways to do this, to find your calm, and to stop feeling so drained by the world around you.
