## Why You Might Feel Stagnant

Have you ever wondered why you feel stuck or unproductive even though you're constantly adapting and managing different roles? You might be the composed one at work, the capable one at home, and the glue holding your friend group together. You've been giving advice, supporting others, and doing all this so naturally that it feels like just being you.

### The Hidden Cost of Masking

But here’s the catch—there’s a cost to this constant performance. Masking, or performing a version of yourself that's easier for others to accept, is incredibly energy-intensive. For many women with ADHD, this happens so seamlessly that it becomes second nature, almost invisible to themselves. Yet, it drains your energy and can leave you feeling stuck.

## How Masking Develops

You probably started masking early on, after being corrected for being too loud, too scattered, or too much in some way. Over time, managing these parts of yourself to avoid discomfort in others became a survival skill. Eventually, the performance shifts from something you do to something you are.

### Recognizing the Limits

The big question then becomes, how long can you keep this up? When does the mask start to weigh too heavily? Recognizing this is the first step toward change.

## Reconnecting With Your True Self

This week, I want you to try a simple but powerful exercise. Write this sentence without editing or making it presentable:

> "Before I started shaping myself to fit other people's comfort, I was someone who _______."

Let it be raw and true. That person you describe is still inside you, just quiet and waiting.

### Awakening the Giant Within

As Tony Robbins says, it’s time to awaken that giant within—the authentic you who doesn’t need to mask or perform. When you stop hiding behind a version of yourself designed for others’ comfort, your true productivity will emerge naturally.

## Moving Forward

Remember, productivity isn’t just about doing more; it’s about doing what aligns with who you really are. By unmasking and embracing your authentic self, you open the door to genuine growth and fulfillment. Start today by acknowledging the real you and watch how your energy and productivity transform.
