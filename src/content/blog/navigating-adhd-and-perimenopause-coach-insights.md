## Understanding the Intersection of ADHD and Perimenopause

As a coach working with women navigating both ADHD and perimenopause, I've learned just how real and tough this journey can be. It’s not just about forgetfulness or being scatterbrained; it’s about the brain’s chemistry shifting as estrogen levels dip, which impacts memory and focus.

### The Brain’s Double-Edged Sword

The same brain that enables brilliant hyperfocus can struggle with memory lapses during perimenopause because estrogen might just "leave the chat." This hormonal shift can make everyday tasks feel overwhelming, but it’s important to remember this isn’t a personality flaw.

### Early Onset and Unique Challenges

One thing I’ve noticed is that perimenopause can hit earlier for women with ADHD compared to neurotypical women. This means being proactive about understanding your body and symptoms is crucial.

## Practical Tips to Manage Daily Life

Living with ADHD and perimenopause means you might find yourself making coffee multiple times because you forgot you already did. Here are some practical strategies that can help:

### Consistency is Key

Try to put items like your coffee mug or medication in the same place every time. One client shared how moving her medication from the car to her purse caused her to forget taking it because she couldn’t get herself to move it back. Her husband’s help in keeping the medicine in the car was a game changer.

### Managing Energy and Sleep

Feeling exhausted by mid-afternoon is common. Hormonal changes involving estrogen, testosterone, and progesterone can disrupt sleep. Some women find relief with progesterone at night, bioidentical hormone implants, or estrogen creams and patches. These are examples from my clients and my own experience with perimenopause.

## Seeking Support and Giving Yourself Grace

If you’re struggling, remember that coaching or consulting with your doctor, especially an OBGYN, can provide the support you need. Don’t wait too long to seek help because early intervention can make a big difference.

### When to Reach Out

If you’re facing challenges with task initiation, emotional regulation, or other ADHD-related struggles in your business or daily life, connecting with a coach can offer tailored strategies and encouragement.

### Be Kind to Yourself

Above all, give yourself grace. These symptoms are part of a complex hormonal and neurological experience, not a reflection of your worth or abilities.

## Final Thoughts

ADHD and perimenopause together create a unique set of challenges, but with understanding, practical habits, and the right support, you can navigate this phase with resilience and hope. If you’re an entrepreneur or someone managing these symptoms daily, know you’re not alone—and help is available.
