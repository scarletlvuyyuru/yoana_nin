\## Understanding the Admin Chaos in Your Business



Many business owners and entrepreneurs face the challenge of inbox and admin chaos. This often happens because operations and administrative work tend to leak into every part of the business. Why does this happen?



\### The Role of ADHD in Task Management



ADHD prioritizes interest over importance, which means unbounded tasks tend to expand and take over your time. This makes it difficult to focus on what truly matters.



\### The Danger of Unbounded Tasks



When tasks aren’t clearly defined or prioritized, they grow and consume your day, leaving important work undone.



\## The Solution: A Daily Admin Power Hour



To regain control, you need to create a daily admin power hour during a low energy time of your day. This focused time helps you manage your tasks effectively.



\### The Three-Step Checklist



1. \*\*Triage:\*\* Quickly sort through your tasks to understand what needs attention.

2. \*\*Decide:\*\* Determine which tasks are important and which can wait.

3. \*\*Schedule:\*\* Plan when to complete the important tasks.



\### Batch and Automate



After scheduling, batch repetitive tasks together and set up automated reminders. This reduces the mental load and keeps you on track.



\## Why This Matters



Without this structure, it’s easy to get lost in a sea of less important tasks. You might find yourself saying, “We have to do this and this and this,” but the time slips away without addressing the most important priorities.



\## Getting Support



Life coaching and guidance can help you stay on track and focus on the big picture. If you’re ready to take control of your admin chaos and prioritize effectively, connecting with a coach can make a real difference.
