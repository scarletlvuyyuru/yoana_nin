## Understanding the Challenge of Boring Tasks

Many high-achieving women I work with share the same struggle: avoiding boring tasks that seem lifeless and lack urgency. These tasks have no pressing deadline, so our brains naturally slide past them, seeking more stimulating activities instead. It’s important to recognize that these tasks won’t magically become interesting—pretending they will only wastes your energy.

## Making the Task Exist

### Find Your Low-Friction Window

The goal is to make the boring task exist in your day. Start by identifying a 20-minute window when your resistance to doing the task is at its lowest. This isn’t necessarily when you have the most energy, but when the urge to avoid the task is weakest. For many, this sweet spot is mid-morning, after breakfast and coffee.

### Prepare the Day Before

The day before you plan to tackle the task, spend two minutes setting it up. This means pulling up documents, printing forms, writing down passwords, or laying out any materials you’ll need. By removing these tiny friction points ahead of time, you make it easier for future you to start the task since it will already feel half done.

## Executing the Task Step-by-Step

### Use a Visible Timer

Set a visible timer for how long you think the task will take, plus an extra 10 minutes. This helps you commit to the time without feeling rushed.

### Break It Into Parts

Do the first 30% of the task completely. You might want to take a short five-minute break afterward, but it’s not required. Then, come back and complete another 30%. Breaking the task into manageable chunks makes it less daunting.

### Celebrate Your Wins

When you finish, tell someone about it. Your brain craves social reinforcement for boring wins; without it, these accomplishments don’t register as real victories. Sharing your progress helps solidify the win and motivates you to keep going.

## Why Starting Now Matters

Remember, the task remains the same size whether you do it now or later. But the guilt and pressure grow bigger every day you wait. So don’t delay—start right now and watch how these small steps make a big difference.
