\## Understanding ADHD Relationship Patterns in Business



If you have ADHD or are in a relationship with someone who does, it’s important to recognize how those relationship habits can spill over into your business life. These patterns don’t just stay in your personal world—they can literally sabotage your business productivity.



\### The Role of Rejection Sensitivity



One key pattern to be aware of is rejection sensitivity. In relationships, this sensitivity might cause you to overthink a tone, a pause, or a text message. While this might seem like just an emotional response, in business, it can have a much bigger impact. It often keeps you from selling, pitching, or putting yourself out there because you’re worried about how others might perceive you.



\### Why This Matters for Your Business



When rejection sensitivity stops you from reaching out or making offers, it directly affects your income and growth. This is especially true if your partner also has ADHD and these patterns are playing out between you two. The stress and misunderstandings can ripple into your business decisions and productivity.



\## The Impact on Relationships and Income



Many couples don’t realize how these patterns influence both their relationship and their business. Over time, if these issues aren’t addressed, they can cause serious strain, even leading to separation or divorce. The connection between your personal and professional life is stronger than you might think.



\### Recognizing the Patterns



Acknowledging that these habits exist is the first step. Whether it’s you or your partner with ADHD, understanding how rejection sensitivity and communication patterns affect your work can help you take control.



\### The Importance of Communication



Open discussions about these challenges are crucial. Without talking about how these patterns impact your business and relationship, the cycle continues, and so do the negative effects.



\## Finding Common Ground and Moving Forward



The good news is that change is possible. It takes some work and a willingness to find common ground with your partner.



\### Working Together



By collaborating and addressing these patterns as a team, you can reduce their impact on your business productivity and relationship health.



\### Getting Support



If you’re struggling to navigate these challenges, connecting with someone who understands ADHD and relationship dynamics can make a big difference. I’m here to help you and your partner find strategies that work.



Remember, ADHD-related patterns don’t have to hold you back. With awareness and effort, you can improve both your relationship and your business success.
